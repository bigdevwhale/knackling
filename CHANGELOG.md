## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-25

- The tasks and challenges sheets can always be closed: a cross in the corner, and a strip of the screen above stays free to tap (#6).
- Project progress can be typed in exactly, fractions too (12.5%); the slider moves by one percent, not five (#2).
- A project can count stages instead of percent: 31 stages for a month's challenge, each ticked on its own (#4).
- Projects can wait in the plans: a kit or a pattern for later, started with one tap (#1).
- A hobby from the list can be taken more than once under its own name (English and Spanish), and embroidery is cross or satin stitch, with its own challenges (#3).
- The «practise before noon» task comes only on Saturdays and Sundays (#5).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-19

First release.

- An attic workshop where every hobby has its own apprentice: 40 preset hobbies with their own creatures and 15 free apprentices for hobbies of your own.
- Sessions by timer (kept in the notification shade) or logged afterwards, with amounts, how it went, a photo, a note and a project.
- Ten ranks from hatchling to legend at ten thousand hours; a golden glow for the high ranks.
- A challenge of the day from every apprentice (160 in all), projects with progress and a showcase wall.
- The ledger: a year quilt, hours per hobby, when you practise, twelve weeks, streaks.
- Daily and weekly tasks with chests, sparks, a shop for the workshop, a shine elixir, more than eighty patches.
- Postcards as posts or stories. A backup file with photos. The Lingvale bridge. Russian and English.
