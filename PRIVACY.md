# Privacy

Knackling has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Your hobbies, sessions, notes, photos of your work, projects and settings stay on your phone, in the app's own storage. Android's backup may keep a copy, as with any app, if you turned it on.

Photos come from the phone's own camera app or from the gallery, and only when you pick them; the app keeps a smaller copy of each.

Notifications are used for a running session's clock and, if you leave them on, for a gentle nudge when an apprentice misses its hobby. Postcards and the backup file leave the phone only when you share or save them yourself.

Lingvale, the StillCraft village app, can read your apprentices and days from Knackling when it is installed on the same phone. It is the only app allowed to ask, and nothing leaves the phone.
